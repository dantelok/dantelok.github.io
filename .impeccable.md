## Design Context

### Users
The primary audience is mathematically curious AI and research readers visiting Dante Lok's personal site. They are comfortable with calculus and technical notation but should not need prior training in algebraic geometry. They come to understand a research idea, verify the central claim, and leave with an honest sense of its scope and limitations.

### Brand Personality
Scholarly, curious, and candid. The writing should make difficult mathematics approachable without flattening it, distinguish verified facts from interpretation, and sound like a thoughtful research colleague rather than a publication or marketing page.

### Aesthetic Direction
A restrained editorial research notebook: warm serif typography, generous reading space, quiet blue links, thin rules, and carefully typeset mathematics. Preserve the existing light/dark themes and the site's spare visual language. Avoid generic card grids, decorative gradients, loud AI imagery, and effects that compete with long-form reading.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with intuition, then state the exact mathematics.
2. Make verification visible: assumptions, calculations, scope, and sources should be easy to inspect.
3. Use diagrams and emphasis only when they clarify a real relationship.
4. Keep long-form reading calm, responsive, accessible, and typographically disciplined.
5. Treat AI attribution and unsettled provenance with precision rather than hype.
